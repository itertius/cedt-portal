# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want participation in CEDT Hub to be harassment-free for everyone, whatever their age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information, including student data, without permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, trolling, or other conduct that is inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/itertius/cedt-portal/security/advisories/new) or a
direct message to [@itertius](https://github.com/itertius). Reports are kept confidential.
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may ban contributors who break this code.
